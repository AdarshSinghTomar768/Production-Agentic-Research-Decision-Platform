# AI Services Campaign Playbook (Internal — GTM)

## Campaign thesis

Mid-market operations leaders are under board pressure to show AI progress but
lack internal capability. We win by selling certainty: a small, fixed-scope
first step with a measurable business outcome, then expanding.

## Targeting criteria (ICP)

- Revenue $200M–$1B, asset-heavy operations (logistics, manufacturing,
  insurance back office).
- A named executive sponsor with a modernization mandate (COO, VP Ops, VP SC).
- At least one of: legacy WMS/ERP, high-volume manual document handling,
  >20k support tickets/month.
- No incumbent AI consultancy engaged (check LinkedIn, press releases).

## Outreach angles ranked by historical reply rate

1. "Your own numbers" angle: reference a quantified pain from public filings
   or job postings (reply rate 18%).
2. Peer benchmark: "three of your peers did X last quarter" (14%).
3. Generic capabilities overview (3%) — do not use.

## Qualification checklist before proposing

- [ ] Named KPI owner identified?
- [ ] Data access realistically obtainable in <4 weeks?
- [ ] Budget authority confirmed (not just interest)?
- [ ] Success metric agreed in writing?

## Disqualification guidance

Walk away when: the ask is a free multi-month POC, there is no executive
sponsor, or the "AI initiative" is actually a hiring plan for an internal team.
Opportunity cost of bad-fit pursuits exceeded $300k of diverted senior time
last year.
