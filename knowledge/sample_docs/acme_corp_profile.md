# Acme Corp — Account Profile (Internal)

**Industry:** Mid-market logistics & fulfillment
**Headquarters:** Chicago, IL
**Employees:** ~1,400
**Revenue:** est. $380M ARR
**Key systems:** SAP ERP, custom WMS (10-year-old Java), Salesforce, Tableau

## Business context

Acme operates 12 distribution centers across the US Midwest and Southeast.
Margins have been compressed by rising last-mile costs for three consecutive
quarters. The new COO (joined Q2) has a public mandate to "modernize operations
with automation" and mentioned AI explicitly in the all-hands.

## Known pain points

- Forecasting: demand planning is spreadsheet-driven; stockouts cost an
  estimated $4–7M annually per internal ops review (2024).
- Customer service: 60k tickets/month; average first response 11 hours;
  no deflection tooling in place.
- Document processing: bills of lading and customs paperwork are manually
  keyed by a 14-person back-office team in Memphis.

## Buying signals

- Posted a "Director of Automation" role in March.
- Attended Gartner Supply Chain Symposium; sessions on GenAI shortlisted.
- Salesforce contract renewal due in 5 months (budget cycle window).

## Relationship history

No active engagement. One discovery call held in January with their VP of
