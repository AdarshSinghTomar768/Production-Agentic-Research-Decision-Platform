# Engagement Post-Mortems — AI Services (Internal)

## Post-mortem: Meridian Foods (2025) — demand forecasting

**Scope:** 10-week fixed-fee assessment + pilot on 2 product lines.
**Outcome:** Forecast MAPE reduced 31% in pilot; expanded to full-line rollout
($1.4M contract).
**What worked:** We got read-only ERP access in week one because we asked for a
named data owner during scoping. Executive sponsor presented pilot results to
their board herself.
**What failed:** Underestimated SAP data extraction effort; ate 3 weeks of
margin. Always price SAP integrations at 1.5x standard.

## Post-mortem: Cortell Insurance (2024) — claims document AI

**Scope:** Claims intake automation POC.
**Outcome:** POC succeeded technically; deal stalled at $80k.
**What failed:** Sponsor was a director without budget authority; procurement
injected a security review that took 11 weeks. Lesson: confirm budget authority
in the first call or disqualify.

## Post-mortem: Harborline Logistics (2025) — service copilot

**Scope:** Support-ticket triage and drafting assistant, 8 weeks.
**Outcome:** Deflection of 22% within 60 days; signed managed-service retainer.
**What worked:** Weekly office hours with the ops team created internal
champions who sold upward for us.
**Transferable metric:** Mid-market logistics buyers respond to deflection and
first-response-time metrics, not model-quality metrics.

## Pricing patterns that closed

- Fixed-fee assessment ($40–70k) converting to implementation (avg 6.2x
  assessment value in follow-on revenue across last 9 engagements).
- Avoid T&M for first project; it signals risk to mid-market CFOs.
